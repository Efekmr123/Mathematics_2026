# Exercise 1 Solution: Matrix Size and Entries

## 1. Result
- Size of A: $2 \times 3$
- Size of B: $3 \times 2$
- Entries: $a_{12} = -1$, $a_{23} = 5$, $b_{21} = -2$, $b_{32} = 1$
- Second row of A: $(0, 4, 5)$
- First column of B: $\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -2 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}$

## 2. Main Steps
1. Count rows and columns: $A$ has 2 rows and 3 columns ($2 \times 3$). $B$ has 3 rows and 2 columns ($3 \times 2$).
2. Locate entry $a_{ij}$ at row $i$, column $j$:
   - $a_{12}$ (row 1, col 2) $= -1$
   - $a_{23}$ (row 2, col 3) $= 5$
   - $b_{21}$ (row 2, col 1) $= -2$
   - $b_{32}$ (row 3, col 2) $= 1$
3. Extract second row of $A$: $(0, 4, 5)$.
4. Extract first column of $B$: $\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -2 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}$.

## 3. Explanation
Matrix sizes are given as (number of rows) $\times$ (number of columns). Entry indices $a_{ij}$ refer to row $i$ and column $j$.

## 4. Verification
- $A$ matrix has 2 horizontal rows and 3 vertical columns $\rightarrow 2 \times 3$.
- $B$ matrix has 3 horizontal rows and 2 vertical columns $\rightarrow 3 \times 2$.
